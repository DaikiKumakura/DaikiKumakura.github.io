How these topics are calculated

TF-IDF vectors use English titles and summaries (65%) and Latin-script words from original article bodies (35%). Bibliographies, code, equations, English stop words and publication-format terms are excluded. Each article connects to up to three nearest neighbors with weighted cosine similarity ≥ 0.08; the union forms the network. Louvain community detection uses resolution 1 and seed 20261027. Labels are the three most distinctive title terms, scored against the rest of the corpus using title/summary TF-IDF centroids.
