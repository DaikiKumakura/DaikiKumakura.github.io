## Definition

**Project Optimus** is an initiative of the Oncology Center of Excellence of the US Food and Drug Administration. It aims to improve how doses are selected in oncology drug development. Historically the dose was often the maximum tolerated dose from early cytotoxic-drug trials; for targeted therapies and antibody drugs the dose with the best balance of benefit and risk may be lower.

## What it asks for

Sponsors are encouraged to compare more than one dose, with randomized dose-finding where appropriate, using exposure, efficacy, safety and tolerability together ([exposure–response analysis](exposure-response.html)) before settling on the dose to take forward.

## On this site

The [sotorasib analysis](../writing/sotorasib-dose-comparison-en.html) uses the arm-level data of a randomized 960 mg versus 240 mg comparison and shows how the choice depends on the weight given to benefit and harm. The [ifinatamab deruxtecan analysis](../writing/b7h3-adc-dose-information.html) asks whether another dose comparison would be informative, and the [borrowing analysis](../writing/borrowing-prior-dose-selection-en.html) asks how much of an existing drug's dose comparison a new drug can borrow. None of them recommends a dose.
