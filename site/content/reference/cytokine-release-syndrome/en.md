## Definition

**Cytokine release syndrome (CRS)** is a systemic inflammatory reaction that follows strong activation of immune cells, with release of cytokines. It occurs with T-cell engaging bispecific antibodies and CAR-T cells. Typical signs are fever and, when more severe, low blood pressure and low oxygen; severity is graded, commonly with the ASTCT consensus grading.

## Link to dosing

CRS is most common after the first dose of a [T-cell engager](t-cell-engager.html). [Step-up dosing](step-up-dosing.html) starts with a low dose to lower the first concentration peak and increases it over the first cycle.

## What PK can and cannot say

A population PK model can show how the first dose, the step size and the interval change exposure. It does not by itself tell how much less CRS occurs: that needs a validated link from PK to receptor occupancy and from occupancy to CRS.
