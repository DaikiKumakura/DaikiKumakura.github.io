Uses quantitative models of drug exposure, response and variability to support drug development decisions ([FDA. Division of Pharmacometrics.](https://www.fda.gov/about-fda/cder-offices-and-divisions/division-pharmacometrics)).

A dose-selection analysis can estimate a population PK model from measured concentrations, simulate exposure under candidate regimens, and evaluate efficacy and safety against those exposures. Each step needs its own evidence: good concentration predictions alone cannot establish a benefit–risk balance.
