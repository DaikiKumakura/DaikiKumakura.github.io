PBPK describes physiology and drug movement. Population PK estimates typical kinetics and variability from pooled observations ([FDA. Physiologically Based Pharmacokinetic Analyses: Format and Content. 2018.](https://www.fda.gov/media/101469/download), [FDA. Population Pharmacokinetics. 2022.](https://www.fda.gov/media/128793/download)).

To predict the impact of a strong CYP3A inhibitor that was not tested clinically, a PBPK model may use information about the enzyme pathway and inhibitor. To estimate between-patient clearance variation from sparse clinical samples, an NLME model can pool observations. The approaches can be combined when an organ-based model is estimated using population data.
