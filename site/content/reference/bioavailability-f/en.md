## Definition

**Bioavailability (F)** is the fraction of an administered dose that reaches the systemic circulation unchanged. For an intravenous dose F = 1. It is estimated by comparing the AUC after an oral dose with the AUC after an intravenous dose, corrected for the dose.

## Apparent parameters

If only oral data are available, F cannot be separated from the clearance and the volume. What can be estimated are the **apparent clearance CL/F** and the **apparent volume V/F**. This is a case of [structural identifiability](structural-identifiability.html): F, CL and V are not identifiable separately, whatever the number of samples.

## On this site

The [warfarin PK/PD analysis](../writing/warfarin-pkpd.html) uses an oral one-compartment model and states that bioavailability cannot be separated from clearance or volume with these oral data, so apparent CL/F and V/F are reported.
