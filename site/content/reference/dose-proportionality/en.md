## Definition

An exposure metric is dose proportional when it rises by the same factor as the dose over a stated range. The power model log(exposure) = α + β log(dose) fits the slope β; β = 1 means proportionality.

## How it is used

A common approach judges the confidence interval of β against bounds derived from the highest-to-lowest dose ratio r: 1 + ln(0.8)/ln(r) to 1 + ln(1.25)/ln(r). Dose-normalized AUC and Cmax across dose levels are shown alongside it.

## Common pitfalls

- Proportionality holds only inside the tested range; extrapolating beyond it needs separate evidence.
- Saturable absorption, saturable clearance or target-mediated disposition produce slopes different from 1, and a wide interval from a small study may be uninformative rather than reassuring.
