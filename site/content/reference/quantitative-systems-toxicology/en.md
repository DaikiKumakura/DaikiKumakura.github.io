## Definition

Quantitative systems toxicology (QST) links drug exposure to mechanisms of biological injury in a quantitative, time-dependent model of a tissue or organ. Examples are DILIsym for drug-induced liver injury and models for cardiac and kidney effects.

## How it is used

A QST model describes injury and recovery over time, so it can distinguish schedules with the same AUC and extend an exposure margin with mechanism. Inputs often come from in vitro assays, such as mitochondrial toxicity, bile-acid transporter inhibition and reactive metabolite formation, scaled to the exposure in the tissue.

## Common pitfalls

- Using plasma exposure when the injury depends on tissue exposure.
- Treating mechanisms with little supporting evidence as established; test predictions against independent clinical or animal findings.
