## Definition

With repeated dosing, the drug accumulates until the amount entering the body in one dosing interval equals the amount eliminated in it. From then on, the concentration–time profile repeats from one interval to the next. This is **steady state**.

## Time to steady state

For linear PK it is reached after about four to five [half-lives](half-life.html), whatever the dose. Drugs with half-lives of weeks therefore take months.

## Steady-state measures

- **Cmax,ss:** the peak within a steady-state interval ([Cmax](cmax-peak-concentration.html)).
- **Ctrough,ss (Cmin,ss):** the lowest concentration ([Ctrough](ctrough-trough-concentration.html)).
- **Cavg,ss:** the average concentration, the dose rate divided by clearance ([AUC](auc-area-under-the-curve.html)).
