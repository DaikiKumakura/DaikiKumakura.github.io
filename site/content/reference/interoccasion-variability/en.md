## Definition

Interoccasion variability (IOV) is the variation of an individual's PK parameter between prespecified occasions, such as dosing visits or the periods of a crossover study. It is modeled as an additional random effect for each occasion, with its own variance, next to the inter-individual variability.

## How it is used

IOV matters when the same patient is observed on several occasions, as in crossover and repeated-dose studies, and for how far a dose can be individualized: an individual's earlier clearance predicts a later one only as well as IOV allows.

## Common pitfalls

- Ignoring IOV can inflate the estimated residual error or inter-individual variability.
- Estimating IOV needs enough data from each individual on more than one occasion; with sparse data it may not be identifiable.
