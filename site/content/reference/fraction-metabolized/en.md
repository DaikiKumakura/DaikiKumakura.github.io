## Definition

The fraction metabolized by a pathway, fm, is the share of total clearance carried by that pathway (for example fm,CYP3A) under defined conditions. It is estimated from mass-balance studies, from reaction phenotyping in vitro or from interaction studies.

## How it is used

fm sets the upper limit of an interaction. In a simplified linear model, complete inhibition of a pathway with fm raises AUC by at most 1/(1 − fm); fm = 0.75 gives four-fold. It is therefore central to deciding whether an interaction study with an inhibitor is needed.

## Common pitfalls

- fm of an enzyme is not the fraction of the dose excreted as a metabolite.
- The simple limit assumes unchanged bioavailability and fixed other pathways; gut metabolism and active transport can change the result.
