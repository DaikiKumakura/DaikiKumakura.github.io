A PK/PD model connects the concentration over time to a pharmacological response ([FDA. Exposure–Response Relationships: Study Design, Data Analysis, and Regulatory Applications. 2003.](https://www.fda.gov/regulatory-information/search-fda-guidance-documents/exposure-response-relationships-study-design-data-analysis-and-regulatory-applications)).

A direct Emax model assigns the same effect to the same concentration. If concentration peaks at hour 1 but a biomarker peaks at hour 6, serial measurements can help distinguish a delay in drug equilibration from slow biomarker turnover. Both can produce a delay, so its presence alone does not identify the mechanism.
