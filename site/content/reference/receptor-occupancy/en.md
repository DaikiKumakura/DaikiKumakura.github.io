## Definition

**Receptor occupancy (RO)** is the fraction of the target receptors that are bound by the drug. For simple, reversible, one-to-one binding at equilibrium, **RO = C / (C + Kd)**, where C is the free drug concentration and Kd the dissociation constant: at C = Kd, half of the receptors are occupied.

## Competition

If another ligand, or residual drug from a previous dose, also binds the target, binding is competitive and the occupancy by the new dose is lower at the same concentration. Models of step-up dosing use this to describe how earlier doses affect the occupancy by later ones.

## Role in PK/PD

Occupancy is a step between the concentration and the pharmacological effect. It can link PK to a response such as [cytokine release syndrome](cytokine-release-syndrome.html) only if each link is supported by data.
