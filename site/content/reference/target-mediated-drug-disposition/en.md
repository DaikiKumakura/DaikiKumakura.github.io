## Definition

**Target-mediated drug disposition (TMDD)** is the situation in which binding of the drug to its pharmacological target, with internalization and degradation of the complex, contributes to the drug's elimination and distribution. It is typical for antibodies against abundant, internalizing targets.

## Nonlinear PK

The binding capacity of the target is limited. At low concentrations a large share of the drug is cleared through the target, so clearance is high; at higher concentrations the target saturates and clearance falls toward the linear, non-specific value. Clearance, and therefore the AUC per dose, depends on dose.

## Models

A full TMDD model describes free drug, free target and the complex. Approximations replace it when the binding is fast: a quasi-steady-state model, or a Michaelis–Menten (nonlinear) elimination term.
