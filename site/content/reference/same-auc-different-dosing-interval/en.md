Equal exposure over the same time window can coexist with different peak and trough concentrations ([FDA. Exposure–Response Relationships: Study Design, Data Analysis, and Regulatory Applications. 2003.](https://www.fda.gov/regulatory-information/search-fda-guidance-documents/exposure-response-relationships-study-design-data-analysis-and-regulatory-applications)).

For an IV bolus one-compartment model with CL = 1 L/h and V = 10 L, compare 12 mg every 12 h with 24 mg every 24 h. Both give steady-state AUC over 24 h of 24 mg·h/L. Using Cmax = (dose/V)/(1 − exp(−CL × interval/V)) and Ctrough = Cmax × exp(−CL × interval/V), the peaks are about 1.72 and 2.64 mg/L, and troughs 0.52 and 0.24 mg/L. Clinical interpretation requires evidence for the exposure measure linked to the endpoint.
