## Definition

**Exposure–response (E–R) analysis** relates a measure of exposure ([AUC](auc-area-under-the-curve.html), [Ctrough](ctrough-trough-concentration.html) or [Cmax](cmax-peak-concentration.html)) to a clinical endpoint of efficacy or safety. It is used to judge whether a dose or a dose change would matter, and to support dose selection.

## Pitfalls

- **Confounding:** for antibodies, patients with more severe disease often have higher clearance, so lower exposure and a worse outcome, which makes the relationship look steeper than the causal effect of exposure.
- **Flat relationships:** when response barely changes across the exposure range, a small change in exposure is unlikely to matter, but the range studied limits this conclusion.
- **Choice of metric:** different metrics (trough or average) can give different conclusions for regimens with different intervals.
