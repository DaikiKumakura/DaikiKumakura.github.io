## Definition

**Cmax** is the highest concentration observed or predicted after a dose; the time it occurs is **Tmax**. At steady state it is **Cmax,ss**. For an infusion, the peak is at the end of the infusion, so a longer infusion lowers Cmax.

## Dose, volume and peak

After an intravenous bolus the initial concentration is roughly the dose divided by the central volume of distribution, so the peak scales with dose and falls with a larger volume. Lengthening the dosing interval at a constant dose rate raises Cmax and lowers [Ctrough](ctrough-trough-concentration.html).
