## Definition

**Step-up dosing** (also called priming or ramp-up dosing) starts treatment with a low dose and increases it in steps, usually within the first cycle, until the target (maintenance) dose is reached. It is used for T-cell engaging bispecific antibodies, for which a high first dose can cause cytokine release syndrome (CRS).

## Example

In the US intravenous product of mosunetuzumab, 1, 2 and 60 mg are given on Cycle 1 Days 1, 8 and 15, 60 mg on Cycle 2 Day 1, and 30 mg every 21 days from Cycle 3.

## What PK can say

A smaller initial dose lowers the first concentration peak, and a population PK model can quantify how the first dose, the second dose and the interval change exposure. But a lower blood concentration does not tell how much less CRS occurs: that needs a link from PK to receptor occupancy and from occupancy to CRS, and public information supports each step to a different degree.
