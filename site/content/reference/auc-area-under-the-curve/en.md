## Definition

**AUC** is the area under the drug concentration–time curve. It measures how much drug the body is exposed to over time, in units such as mg·h/L. **AUC0–τ** is the AUC over one dosing interval τ; **AUC0–∞** is the AUC after a single dose extrapolated to infinity.

## Link to dose and clearance

For linear PK, the AUC after an intravenous dose is the dose divided by clearance. At steady state, AUC0–τ is the dose per interval divided by clearance, so the average concentration is **Cavg,ss = AUC0–τ / τ**, that is, the dose rate divided by clearance.
