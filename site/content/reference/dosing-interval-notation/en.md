## Meaning

In dosing regimens, **Q** stands for the Latin *quaque*, "every". A number and a unit of time follow it.

| Notation | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| QD | once daily |
| BID | twice daily |
| Q8H | every 8 hours |
| Q1W (QW) | every week |
| Q2W | every 2 weeks |
| Q3W | every 3 weeks |
| Q4W | every 4 weeks |
| Q6W | every 6 weeks |

## Examples from antibody dosing

- Pembrolizumab: 200 mg Q3W, or 400 mg Q6W.
- Nivolumab: 240 mg Q2W, or 480 mg Q4W.
- Weight-based forms: 2 mg/kg Q3W or 3 mg/kg Q2W, where the dose scales with body weight.

Doubling the interval while doubling the dose keeps the average exposure the same, but the trough falls and the peak rises; see [Ctrough](ctrough-trough-concentration.html) and the [anti-PD-1 exposure explorer](../tools/anti-pd1-exposure.html).
