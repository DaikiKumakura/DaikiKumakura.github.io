## Definition

An exposure metric is the quantity summarizing the concentration–time profile that is related to an outcome, such as Cmax, Ctrough, AUC, average concentration or time above a threshold. It is defined together with its time window, for example after the first dose or at steady state.

## How it is used

Choose the metric and window from the mechanism and the timing of the outcome: a peak-related event points to Cmax, a sustained target effect to trough or time above a threshold, and a cumulative effect to AUC. Fix this choice before looking at the outcome data.

## Common pitfalls

- Cmax, trough and AUC are often highly correlated, so the data may not distinguish them.
- Exposure depends on clearance, which can depend on disease severity; an exposure–outcome association can partly reflect that. See confounding in exposure–response analysis.
