Unbound concentration is the fraction of total drug concentration that is free of binding in the sampled matrix ([FDA. Physiologically Based Pharmacokinetic Analyses: Format and Content. 2018.](https://www.fda.gov/media/101469/download), [ICH. S3A: The Assessment of Systemic Exposure in Toxicity Studies. 1994.](https://www.fda.gov/media/71990/download)).

If the fraction unbound is 0.05 and total plasma concentration is 20 mg/L, unbound plasma concentration is 1 mg/L at that condition. The conversion assumes the fraction is known for the same matrix and concentration range. Transport, tissue binding and metabolism can make the relationship to unbound concentration at a tissue target more complex.
