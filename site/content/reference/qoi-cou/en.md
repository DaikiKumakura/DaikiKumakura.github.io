## Definition

- **Question of interest (QOI):** the specific question that a modeling or data analysis is meant to answer, written down before the method is chosen.
- **Context of use (COU):** how the result will be used, that is, what decision it will inform and what it will not be used for.

Both terms are used in model-informed drug development (MIDD) to say how much credibility a model needs: the same model can be adequate for one context of use and inadequate for another.

## Why state them first

A question fixed after seeing the results can be tuned to whatever the data happen to show. A context of use also limits the claim in advance. An analysis meant to prioritize further research does not have to meet the standard of one that sets a dose, and it must not be read as one.

## How the site uses them

Every analysis on this site states its QOI and COU near the top. For example, the [early tumor change and survival analysis](../writing/tumor-dynamics-survival.html) has research prioritization as its context of use, and says that treatment switching, dosing and surrogate qualification are not assessed. The [mosunetuzumab reimplementation](../writing/mosunetuzumab-public-pk-en.html) limits itself to model reimplementation and regimen comparison for research and learning.
